READING: Bhagavad Gita I: 1:

B:	The term ‘dharma’ has several meanings: 1) beingness (unfolding ourselves), 2) virtues, 3) religion, 4) duty. Beingness is the main meaning of dharma—like in fire, the heat is its beingness. Without heat, the fire loses its reality. In human beings, beingness is  apavarga or to achieve the liberated state. From where they originally came? All of the qualities, activities, thoughts, etc., which are conducive to liberation are also dharma. So this field of battle was named ‘field of dharma.’ Kurus owned this land, plowed this land, so he wanted this land.
M:	That’s why it was called ‘land of the Kurus.’
B:	One time when he was plowing the God Indra came and saw his austerity and gave his blessing and said, “this place will be an auspicious place for virtuous people and  yogis who will come to meditate here and get liberated. Those who will die in battle here  will also get liberated.” After that Kurus stopped plowing the land and this land became [dharmakṣetra] or land of virtue. Kuru term comes from the word ‘kri’ which means action. In this sense [Kurukṣetra] means ‘land of action.’ After the fall of [Bhīṣma], Pitamata, by the commander in chief of the [Kaurava] army, [Sañjaya] who was in the palace and could see the war, went to [Dhṛtarāṣṭra] to tell him about [Bhīṣma]’s fall. At that time, [Dhṛtarāṣṭra] asked him this question, “what happened on the battlefield?” which is the beginning of the Bhagavad [Gīta]. This is the only time [Dhṛtarāṣṭra] is mentioned in the Gita. The 700 verses of the [Gīta] are answers of [Dhṛtarāṣṭra] question. It was possible that 1) [Dhṛtarāṣṭra] thought his sons are winning the war because their army was much bigger. 2) It was possible that he thought his sons are in the field of dharma and their mind about war was changed. But the field of dharma doesn’t work in everyone in the same way. The [Kaurava] got more excited to fight. But Arjuna started seeing futility in the war. In a graveyard, when people take a body to bury, some start thinking about mortality of the body and some start playing cards. Same place is exciting  people’s [saṃskāra] differently. Arjuna feeling that way in the field of dharma was right, but untimely. Like at the birthday party, everyone rejoices but the birthday person gets depressed; it isn’t timely.
